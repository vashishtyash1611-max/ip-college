General Instructions

Please read these guidelines carefully before applying.

  • Admissions follow the affiliating university's process / entrance test and schedule.
  • Ensure eligibility (generally 50% in qualifying degree with relevant subjects).
  • Reservation as per University / State Government policies.
  • Keep originals + photocopies of certificates, ID and photographs ready.
  • Provide accurate contact details for counselling updates.
  • Fee rules follow the latest notification — contact the college for current details.
