Bachelor of Education — B.Ed.

Regular programme approved by NCTE • 2 units / 200 students • University admission process.

Duration

Regular B.Ed. as per NCTE / university norms.

Eligibility

Generally 50% in Bachelor's/Master's with relevant subjects. Reservation per policy.

Admission

Through university process / entrance test.

Internship

Minimum 30-day teaching internship + practice.

Subjects & training areas

School Subjects

Regional Language / Mother Tongue, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Sciences.

Senior-Secondary Areas

Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology, History, Geography, Political Science, Economics, Commerce.

Supporting Areas

Physical Education, Art, Work Experience, IT Literacy.

Foundation courses

Perspectives on education and psychology.

Methodology courses

Pedagogy of school subjects.

Internship & practice

Skill development and supervised teaching.
