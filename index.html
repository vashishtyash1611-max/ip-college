About the College

Inderprastha Education College — a trusted B.Ed. institution in Haryana

Located at Jind Bypass, Tehsil & District Rohtak, the college is affiliated to Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and run by Arya Shiksha Samiti, Rohtak. Courses follow NCTE guidelines.

  • Quality education & professional work culture for future teachers
  • Human values, discipline, teamwork and personality development
  • Teaching practice, internship and skill-based training
